What retailers should evaluate before selecting software and hardware.

POS and inventory must share accurate products, prices and stock movements. A fast checkout that creates unreliable inventory will move the problem to purchasing and accounting.

Requirements to test

  • Sales, returns, discounts and shift closing
  • Multiple branches, warehouses and transfers
  • Barcodes, units, variants and batch tracking
  • Payment reconciliation and accounting
  • Offline or unstable-connectivity behavior
  • Permissions, overrides and audit history

Demonstrate real exceptions

Test returns without receipts, price overrides, partial payments, damaged stock, inter-branch transfers and end-of-day differences—not only a simple cash sale.
