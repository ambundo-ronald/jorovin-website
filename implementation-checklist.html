ERP implementation
checklist.

Print this page or save it as PDF for your project team.

Discovery

  • ☐ Confirm sponsor, governance and success measures
  • ☐ Map scope, processes, exceptions and controls
  • ☐ Assign process and data owners

Solution and data

  • ☐ Approve modules, roles, workflows and integrations
  • ☐ Clean and map master data
  • ☐ Define migration history and reconciliation totals

Validation

  • ☐ Test complete real-world scenarios
  • ☐ Test permissions, reports and exceptions
  • ☐ Record issues and obtain process-owner approval

People and launch

  • ☐ Train users by role using real tasks
  • ☐ Approve cutover plan, backups and responsibilities
  • ☐ Establish support channels and stabilization metrics
