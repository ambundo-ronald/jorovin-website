What really determines
ERP implementation cost?

A transparent breakdown of the work behind an ERP budget.

ERP implementation cost depends on organisational complexity—not only software licensing. Users, modules, companies, data quality, integrations, customization, training and rollout risk all affect the effort required.

Major cost components

ComponentWhat affects it
DiscoveryNumber of processes, locations and stakeholders
ConfigurationModules, roles, controls and workflows
MigrationSource systems, history and data quality
CustomizationRequirements not met by standard functionality
TrainingUser groups, materials and adoption support
SupportResponse expectations and ongoing improvement

How to obtain a useful estimate

Provide process scope, user counts, companies, integrations, data sources and known exceptions. Treat an instant quote without discovery cautiously.
