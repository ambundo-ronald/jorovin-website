IT consulting connects business strategy with technology decisions. A consultant analyses processes, systems, data and risks before recommending which problems to solve, in what order and with which digital approach.

What an IT consultant should deliver

A useful engagement should produce decisions—not a collection of technical buzzwords. Depending on scope, the output may include a process assessment, system architecture, prioritized roadmap, integration plan, implementation budget and measurable success indicators.

Problems we help businesses examine

  • Repeated manual entry and spreadsheet dependence
  • Disconnected sales, inventory, accounting and customer data
  • Slow reporting and unreliable management information
  • High software costs or overlapping subscriptions
  • Poor digital customer journeys
  • Legacy systems that cannot support growth

How Jorovin operates

  1. Listen: understand goals, constraints and the people affected.
  2. Observe: study real workflows remotely or during a company visit.
  3. Analyse: quantify friction, risk, cost and opportunity.
  4. Recommend: present a clear roadmap ordered by business value.
  5. Deliver: implement directly or support your selected delivery team.

Cost savings and revenue growth

Technology creates savings by reducing rework, errors, delays and unnecessary subscriptions. It supports revenue through faster sales processes, ecommerce, better customer service, mobile access and clearer information about demand. Every recommendation should connect to an observable business outcome.

Frequently asked questions

Do we need to know which software we want?

No. Start with the business problem. Product selection should follow discovery and requirements analysis.

Can consultation happen remotely?

Yes. Jorovin operates remotely and can conduct interviews, workshops and system reviews online. On-site visits are useful when physical workflows must be observed.

Does Jorovin only consult on ERPNext?

No. ERPNext is a major specialization, while our consultation scope also covers websites, ecommerce, POS, mobile applications, integrations and custom SaaS platforms.
