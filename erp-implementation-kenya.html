ERP implementation Kenya

A practical guide to
implementing ERP.

What Kenyan businesses should understand before replacing spreadsheets and disconnected systems with one operational platform.

Reviewed by Jorovin Consulting8 minute readUpdated August 2026

ERP implementation is the process of mapping business operations, configuring a shared system, migrating data, training users and launching new workflows. A successful implementation changes how the organisation works—not only the software it uses.

When does a business need ERP?

ERP becomes valuable when separate spreadsheets and applications make it difficult to trust information, control stock, close accounts, serve customers or understand performance.

  • Teams repeatedly enter the same information.
  • Finance and operations reports do not agree.
  • Stock levels are discovered too late.
  • Approvals depend on calls and messages.
  • Growth requires adding administrative work faster than revenue.

The implementation process

  1. Discovery: document workflows, pain points, controls and goals.
  2. Solution design: select modules, roles, integrations and rollout phases.
  3. Configuration: build the chart of accounts, items, permissions and workflows.
  4. Migration: clean, validate and import master and opening data.
  5. Testing and training: confirm real scenarios with actual users.
  6. Go-live and support: launch with clear ownership and rapid issue handling.

What determines ERP implementation cost?

There is no responsible fixed price without discovery. Cost is primarily driven by company count, users, modules, data quality, integrations, customization, reporting and the amount of training required.

Choosing an implementation partner

Look for a partner that understands accounting and operations as well as software. Ask how they manage scope, test migrations, train users and support the business after launch.

Frequently asked questions

How long does ERP implementation take?

A focused small-business rollout may take weeks, while a multi-company or manufacturing project can take several months. Discovery determines the realistic timeline.

Can ERPNext support a Kenyan business?

ERPNext provides accounting, stock, sales, buying, manufacturing, projects and HR capabilities. Local configuration, process design and integrations should be reviewed for each organisation.

Should we migrate all historical data?

Not always. Many businesses migrate clean master data, opening balances and essential history while preserving the old system for reference.
