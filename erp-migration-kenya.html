Structured migration from spreadsheets and legacy platforms to ERPNext, with mapping, cleaning, testing and reconciliation.

ERP migration is not a simple export and import. Source data must be understood, cleaned, mapped to the new structure and reconciled against agreed control totals.

Migration stages

  1. Inventory: identify source systems, owners and required history.
  2. Mapping: connect old fields and codes to the new data model.
  3. Cleaning: remove duplicates and resolve invalid or incomplete records.
  4. Trial migration: load representative data and test real processes.
  5. Reconciliation: confirm balances, quantities and record counts.
  6. Cutover: freeze sources, migrate final data and approve go-live.

Migration questions

Should we migrate all history?

Not necessarily. The right balance depends on reporting, audit, customer service and data-quality requirements.

Can you migrate from spreadsheets?

Yes. Spreadsheets often require substantial cleaning and standardization before they are safe to import.
