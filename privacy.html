Privacy & Cookie Policy

Effective 16 August 2026

This policy explains how Jorovin Limited handles personal information submitted through our website, business enquiries and optional website analytics.

Information we collect

We may collect your name, work email, telephone number, company, service interests and information included in messages you send us. Our hosting and form providers may also process basic technical information such as IP address and browser details for security and delivery.

How we use information

  • Respond to enquiries and provide requested consultation.
  • Prepare proposals and communicate about potential or active projects.
  • Provide customer support.
  • Understand website performance when analytics consent is granted.
  • Protect our website and forms from abuse.
  • Meet legal, accounting and regulatory obligations.

Forms and service providers

Website enquiries are processed by Web3Forms and delivered to Jorovin. Their processing is governed by their own privacy terms. We do not sell personal information.

Cookies and Google Analytics

With your permission, we use Google Analytics 4 with measurement ID G-610GPN48S9 to understand page visits, enquiry conversions and contact interactions. The analytics script is not loaded until you select “Accept analytics.”

Your preference is stored locally in your browser under jorovin_analytics_consent. Declining analytics does not restrict access to the website. You can reopen Cookie preferences from the footer and change your decision.

Google may process device, browser, approximate location and usage information under its own privacy terms. IP anonymization is requested in our configuration.

Retention and security

We keep enquiry information only for as long as reasonably necessary for communication, service delivery and legal obligations. We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards, but no internet transmission is completely risk-free.

Your choices

You may decline optional analytics and request access, correction or deletion of personal information, subject to applicable legal requirements.
