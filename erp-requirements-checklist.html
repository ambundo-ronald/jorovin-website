ERP requirements
checklist.

Questions to answer before requesting demonstrations or implementation proposals.

A good ERP requirement describes a business outcome, user, trigger, workflow, control and expected result. Avoid copying a vendor’s feature list.

Organisation

  • Companies, branches, currencies and reporting structure
  • User groups, roles and approval authority
  • Implementation sponsors and process owners

Operations

  • Sales, procurement, inventory and fulfilment workflows
  • Accounting, tax, budgets and financial close
  • Manufacturing, projects, assets or service requirements
  • Exceptions, returns, cancellations and corrections

Technology and data

  • Source systems and migration history
  • Required integrations and payment channels
  • Security, backups, audit and access requirements
  • Reports, dashboards and regulatory outputs
