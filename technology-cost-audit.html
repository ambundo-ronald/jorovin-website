Technology cost
audit worksheet.

Look beyond subscription invoices to find the real cost of fragmented systems.

Direct technology spend

  • ☐ List every subscription, license, hosting service and support contract
  • ☐ Record owner, users, monthly cost and renewal date
  • ☐ Identify overlapping or underused capabilities

Manual-work cost

  • ☐ Estimate hours spent re-entering or reconciling information
  • ☐ Record recurring spreadsheet preparation and report delays
  • ☐ Identify approvals dependent on calls and messages

Error and opportunity cost

  • ☐ Stock differences, billing errors, rework and missed deadlines
  • ☐ Lost enquiries, abandoned checkouts and slow customer response
  • ☐ Decisions delayed by unavailable information

Prioritize

Rank each issue by annual cost, risk, customer impact and implementation effort. Start with changes that remove repeated cost or unlock measurable revenue.
