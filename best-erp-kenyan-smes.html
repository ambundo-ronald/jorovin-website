ERP for Kenyan SMEs

The best ERP is the one
your business can use well.

A practical selection framework for growing Kenyan companies.

There is no universal best ERP for every Kenyan SME. The strongest choice fits essential workflows, remains affordable to operate, integrates with the business environment and has dependable implementation support.

Build your shortlist around seven factors

  1. Industry and operational fit
  2. Accounting and reporting requirements
  3. Inventory, branch or manufacturing complexity
  4. Integration needs
  5. User experience and adoption
  6. Five-year ownership cost
  7. Implementation and support capability

Signs you are ready

You have process owners, reliable decision-makers, time for training, data-cleaning capacity and agreed outcomes. Buying software before these conditions exist increases risk.

Questions

Should an SME start with all modules?

Usually not. A phased rollout can reduce risk if the initial phase still forms a coherent operational process.

Is ERPNext a good option?

ERPNext should be shortlisted when its operational modules, open-source approach and implementation ecosystem fit the business.
