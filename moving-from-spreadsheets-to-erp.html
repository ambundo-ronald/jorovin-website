ERP readiness

From spreadsheet dependence
to shared operations.

How to prepare people, processes and data for an ERP transition.

Move to ERP when spreadsheets create conflicting information, repeated entry, weak controls or reporting delays. Do not simply recreate every spreadsheet inside the new system.

Prepare before configuration

  1. List critical spreadsheets and their owners.
  2. Identify duplicate and conflicting sources.
  3. Agree standard customers, items, accounts and units.
  4. Document approvals and exceptions.
  5. Define which history truly needs migration.
  6. Assign process owners and data approvers.

Use a phased transition

Choose a coherent first process, rehearse with real scenarios and reconcile results before removing old tools. Set a clear cutover date and rules for emergency exceptions.

Questions

Can we import spreadsheets directly?

Some data can be imported, but it must first be mapped, cleaned and validated against the ERP structure.

Should spreadsheets disappear completely?

ERP should become the source of truth for controlled transactions. Spreadsheets may remain useful for temporary analysis, not parallel record keeping.
