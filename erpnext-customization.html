Our customization principles

  • Confirm the business requirement first

  • Document scope and expected behavior

  • Test permissions, data and upgrade compatibility

Customization questions

Can too much customization become a problem?

Yes. Unnecessary custom code increases testing, support and upgrade effort. Each change should have a clear owner and business case.

Can you review an existing customized system?

Yes. We can assess custom apps, scripts, workflows, reports and technical debt before recommending improvements.
