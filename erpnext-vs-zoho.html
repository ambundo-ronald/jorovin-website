ERPNext vs Zoho:
different strengths.

How to choose between an integrated operational ERP and a broad suite of cloud business applications.

ERPNext is commonly evaluated as a unified operational ERP across accounting, stock, manufacturing, projects and HR. Zoho offers a broad family of cloud applications. The right choice depends on how deeply operations must be connected and which specific applications your teams need.

Questions that determine fit

QuestionWhy it matters
Do you manage stock or production?Operational depth may matter more than the total number of available apps.
Must accounting share one data model?Integration design affects reconciliation and reporting.
How much customization is expected?Compare configuration, development and long-term maintenance.
Which tools are already adopted?Migration and user change can outweigh licensing differences.

Run workflow demonstrations

Ask both systems to demonstrate quote-to-cash, procurement, reporting and one industry-specific workflow using representative data. Confirm current editions, pricing and capabilities directly with the vendors.

Questions

Is Zoho an ERP?

Zoho provides many business applications. Whether the selected combination meets your definition of ERP depends on the required operational scope and integration.

Which is cheaper?

Compare subscription, implementation, integrations, customization, support and internal administration over several years.
